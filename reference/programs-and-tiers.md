## Tiers

### Base / Community tier (included)
Route non-Premium members here. Includes:
- **Q&A discussion board** (the Q&A Vault method)
- **Communication Lab** (+ the Communication Lab Vault)
- **Monthly live calls**
- **The Classroom** (course modules, below)

### Premium tier (higher paid, 1:1)
Adds, per the Blueprint standards (Source §10) and VIP offer (Source §9):
- **90-Day Repair Blueprint** — custom plan, walked through on a 1:1 Kickoff call, living document
- **Cadence:** ONE Kickoff/Blueprint call + ONE 90-Day Reset call per 90 days (the Reset is a retention anchor in every Blueprint). **Not a monthly 1:1.**
- **Anytime access** via private chat + voice notes (weekly-ish rhythm; ~48h reply), plus the monthly live group calls
- **Hot Seat Clinic** (Premium-only — do NOT mention to non-Premium members)
- **Elevate Hour**, **Implementation & Communication Lab** call

> Naming note: the source uses both **"Premium"** and **"VIP"** for the paid 1:1 tier (the "soft VIP open" upsells into it; Blueprint standards call it "Premium"). They appear to refer to the same paid 1:1 tier. *Confirm with Eti whether Premium and VIP are one tier or distinct.*

## The VIP offer (three parts, Source §9)

1. **Private weekly access** — message Tania directly any week; reply by voice note or writing, usually ~48h.
2. **The 90-Day Blueprint** — custom 1:1 plan.
3. **Customized touchpoints** — plan bends as things shift.

Money posture: no pressure; the one promise is "you'd stop guessing"; never promise reconnection.
