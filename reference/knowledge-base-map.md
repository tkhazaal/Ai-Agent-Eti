## Most operationally critical rules (quick reference)

- **Refund guarantee: REMOVED 2026-09-03.** Do not re-add without Tania's explicit go-ahead. Details + standing rule in [billing-refund-policy](../support/billing-refund-policy.md). (Source §2)
- **TIER RULE:** non-Premium members route only to Q&A board, Communication Lab, or monthly calls. Never mention Hot Seat Clinic unless confirmed Premium. If unsure, default to Q&A board only. (Source §4) See [support & escalation](../rules-and-preferences/support-and-escalation.md).
- **Story before offer:** never lead with price/link/upgrade; get who they're estranged from and how long first. (Source §9)
- **Never promise reconnection / the child's response / a timeframe.** (Source §2, §9 R-014) See [brand guardrails](../rules-and-preferences/brand-guardrails.md).
- **New intro post ≠ resource routing.** A first hello just gets warmth, not resource links. (Source §4)
- **Confidentiality:** never name one member's details in another's reply/Blueprint. (Source §9, §10)
- **Safety disclosures** (sexualized/unsafe content involving a child): remove/restrict public content, follow up privately, route to a professional, keep the member's place in the community open. (Source §4) See [moderation & safety](../rules-and-preferences/moderation-and-safety.md).
- **Voice:** first person for founder-led offers; "God" not "the Lord" for faith language; no AI/clinical phrasing; no em dashes. Full retired/banned phrase list in Source §14b. See [communication style](../rules-and-preferences/communication-style.md).
