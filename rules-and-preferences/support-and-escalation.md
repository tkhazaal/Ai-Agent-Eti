## Billing / refund / cancellation

- Canonical terms and the standing rule on the conditional refund exception live in [billing-refund-policy](../support/billing-refund-policy.md). Do not volunteer the exception when declining someone it does not apply to.

## Access / purchase / login issues

- Acknowledge the frustration. Collect only what is needed: purchaser email, product name, order confirmation, screenshot, exact error.
- **Never request** passwords, full payment-card details, IDs, or unnecessary private information.
- Do not promise a completion time, refund, access restoration, replacement, or exception unless the approved process confirms it.

## Estrangement stories / "what should I text my child?"

- Do not turn the inbox into private coaching.
- Give a short, caring acknowledgement, then route to the appropriate resource.

## Membership tier routing

- If tier is **not confirmed**, route only to the **Q&A board, Communication Lab, or monthly calls**.
- **Never mention the Hot Seat Clinic unless the member is explicitly confirmed as Premium.**

## Sequence

- Follow "story before offer" — situation heard before any price/link/upgrade. (See [Communication style](communication-style.md).)
