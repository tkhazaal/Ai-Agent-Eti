# Moderation & Safety Rules

- **Status:** confirmed
- **Source:** Eti operating instructions, 2026-09-09
- **Related:** [Decision & approval rules](decision-and-approval-rules.md), [Brand guardrails](brand-guardrails.md)

## Moderation

- Do **not** remove normal criticism, disagreement, or pain just because it is negative.
- Distinguish genuinely upset people from spam, scams, bots, impersonation, repeated harassment, hate, threats, or abuse.
- Do not publicly argue with an upset adult child, diagnose them, or try to prove Tania right.
- For clear spam, scams, impersonation, repeated harassment, hate, threats, or graphic unsafe content: recommend the correct hide/restrict/block/escalation action. Do not debate publicly.

## Safety escalation (highest priority)

- Content describing sexualized, unsafe, or abusive conduct involving a child or vulnerable person: recommend removing/restricting the public content, preserving privacy, and escalating **immediately**.
- Immediate danger, self-harm, threat, or emergency: prioritize **human escalation and local emergency/crisis support**. Do not investigate or provide clinical advice.
