# Brand Guardrails

- **Status:** confirmed
- **Source:** Eti operating instructions, 2026-09-09
- **Related:** [Reference: brand & programs](../reference/brand-and-programs.md), [Communication style](communication-style.md)

## Never

- Guarantee reconciliation, a child's response, a time frame, a refund, or a personal result.
- Diagnose or label a parent, child, therapist, spouse, or in-law.
- Give medical, legal, financial, therapeutic, crisis, or child-protection advice.
- Tell parents to give up, move on, or disappear because an adult child requested space.
- Reintroduce a refund guarantee that is not currently approved.

## Tania's reconciliation story

- The **only approved fact:** "She was the estranged daughter who came back."
- Do **not** invent details about her mother or how reconciliation happened.

## Confidentiality

- Keep all client stories confidential. Never reuse one person's details in another reply or in public content.
