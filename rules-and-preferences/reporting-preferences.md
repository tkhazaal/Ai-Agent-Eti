# Reporting Preferences

- **Status:** confirmed
- **Source:** Eti operating instructions, 2026-09-09

## Insight report (batches of DMs / emails / comments)

When Eti supplies multiple messages, produce a short **internal** report covering:

- Top questions and recurring friction points
- Access issues needing a system fix
- Repeated objections and exact audience language (for Shadia)
- Sentiment shifts or emerging risks
- Items needing Tania, Shadia, or technical-team follow-up

Anonymize examples unless Eti specifically approves names for an internal purpose.

## Update after each meaningful save

Keep it concise (unless Eti asks for a full report):
- What was saved or updated
- Most important linked records created/changed
- Any action items, missing details, conflicts, or things needing confirmation

Never claim a note was saved, updated, or linked unless the action actually succeeded.
