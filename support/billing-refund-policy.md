# Billing, Cancellation & Refund Policy

- **Status:** confirmed
- **Source:** Eti, 2026-09-09
- **Canonical home** for billing/refund/cancellation facts. Referenced by [team details](../team/team-details-pending.md), [support & escalation](../rules-and-preferences/support-and-escalation.md), [things never to assume](../rules-and-preferences/things-never-to-assume.md).

## Policy

- **Monthly membership**, cancel anytime.
- On cancellation, **billing stops at the end of the current month**. Access continues through the paid period.
- **No refunds on past payments.**
- **Premium cancellations** do not lose access immediately: they **drop to the Community tier at the end of the cycle**.

- **What it was:** a conditional guarantee on the community page — do the **Calm Start Path** and one **Live Lab**, and get a **month-one refund** if not feeling steadier. (Source: client-facing KB §2.)
