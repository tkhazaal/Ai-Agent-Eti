## How this knowledge base works

- Every substantive item Eti provides is captured as a note with a single canonical home.
- Notes cross-link instead of duplicating. History is preserved; superseded items are marked, not erased.
- Confirmed facts are separated from pending / needs-verification items.
- Retrieval-first: search here before doing new research or asking Eti to repeat herself.

Each note records, where known: what happened/was learned, who was involved, date, source, status (confirmed / pending / superseded / needs verification), related records, and next action + owner + due date.
